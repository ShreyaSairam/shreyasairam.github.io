      Click the floor to drop a wet floor sign. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

      1. Language to tool calls

      A parser turns your sentence into structured calls like move(bed 4, Analyser A). It checks them against lab rules first, so a blood count sent to the chemistry analyser gets corrected. A cloud LLM (Gemini) can plug in through a small worker; if it is off or slow, the local parser takes over.

      2. Planning the route

      Each trip is planned with A* on a grid of the lab. The A* layer shows which cells it explored and the traffic layer shows where the robot spends its time. A wet floor sign, a spill or a nurse in the way triggers a replan from wherever the robot is.

      3. Measuring a shift

      Turnaround time is measured the way labs measure it: from arrival to loading on the analyser. The metrics tab tracks the median and 90th percentile, where the robot's time goes, and how the rack queue builds. Autopilot always takes the sample closest to missing its deadline.
