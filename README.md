## Featured projects

| Project | What it does | Links |
|---|---|---|
| **Genetic Testing Decision Support** | Helps a GP choose the right genetic test from a patient's symptoms, with consent, pseudonymisation, role based access and a SHA-256 chained audit trail. Right gene in the recommended test 99% of the time with 5 symptoms. | [Try it](https://shreyasairam.github.io/genetic-testing/index.html) · [Case study](https://shreyasairam.github.io/work/genetic-testing.html) · [Code](genetic-testing/code) |
| **Churn Prediction: Employees to Bank Customers** | Predicts who is likely to leave and why. Bank: LightGBM on 1.06M real transactions, AUC 0.89. Employees: five models compared on IBM HR data. | [Bank customers](https://shreyasairam.github.io/quiet-exits/index.html) · [Employees](https://shreyasairam.github.io/quiet-exits/employees.html) · [Case study](https://shreyasairam.github.io/work/churn-prediction.html) · [Code](quiet-exits/code) |
| **Turnaround** | Simulates 11,800 hospital lab samples to find where urgent blood tests lose their hour, then tests fixes. Three changes lift the morning from 60% to 83% on time. | [Console](https://shreyasairam.github.io/lab-turnaround/index.html) · [Case study](https://shreyasairam.github.io/work/lab-turnaround.html) · [Code](lab-turnaround) |
| **Out of Phase** | Tests whether batteries that chase the electricity price help or hurt the local grid, with real data from Sydney and Delhi and a linear program optimiser. | [Play](https://shreyasairam.github.io/out-of-phase/index.html) · [Lab](https://shreyasairam.github.io/out-of-phase/lab.html) · [Case study](https://shreyasairam.github.io/work/out-of-phase.html) · [Code](out-of-phase) |
| **Lumen** | Team project, FEIT Hackathon 2026 (SMEC Spot Prize, Top 5). Privacy safe pedestrian counting and shade aware walking routes for Cremorne. | [Live prototype](https://lumen.yuanhaofeng.com/) · [Case study](https://shreyasairam.github.io/work/lumen.html) |
