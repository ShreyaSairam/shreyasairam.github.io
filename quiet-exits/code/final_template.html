What this can't tell you
Old data, one country. A Czech bank, 1993 to 1998. The method transfers; the numbers won't. Reasons are inferred, not known. "Big one-off withdrawal" could be a wedding, a car or a flat. Real bank data would add card spend categories and app activity, and with consent, Account Aggregator data. No record of what the bank did. So this can't prove a response works. That needs a test with a control group. A chosen definition. Money "leaves" when the next 3 months fall below 70% of the usual balance.
