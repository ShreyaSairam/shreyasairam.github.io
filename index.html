Out of Phase

Who does your battery really work for? Big batteries earn money by following the electricity price, which is set for a whole region. The strain on the grid happens at the local substation on your street. I used real data from Sydney and Delhi to test whether the two line up.

42%of days a Sydney substation peak lands near the price peak
24%in Sydney summers, when the grid is most stretched
40%the same check for Delhi

The live demo lets you run a real day yourself: pick Sydney or Delhi and a battery size, then balance profit against the street with a slider or by dragging the release window.

Health AI · Privacy and governance

Genetic Testing Decision Support

Helps a GP choose the right genetic test, with the safeguards a clinic needs: consent before anything runs, pseudonymised data, role based access, an overseas transfer check and a tamper evident audit trail. Switch a safeguard off and see what breaks.

Tested on simulated patients: right gene in the recommended test 99% of the time with 5 symptoms. The checks caught an overconfident label (right 69% of the time) and the fix raised it to 88%.

Churn analytics · Employees and bank customers

Churn Prediction: From Employees to Bank Customers

The same question in two places: who is about to leave, why, and what to do. First on employees (five models compared, from my Melbourne coursework), then on bank customers, whose savings quietly drain away before they go. Both halves answer live as you change the inputs.

Employees: 0.85 AUC on 1,470 staff records. Bank: trained on 1.06 million real transactions, spots 53% of leavers in its top 10% of flags and names the reason correctly 67% of the time, 3 months early.
