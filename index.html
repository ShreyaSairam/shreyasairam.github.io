About me

Structured by habit, flexible by experience

I grew up in Chennai as the person who plans everything: one plus one is two. Moving to Melbourne taught me the other half. Different teams, different cultures, and the lesson that my way is not always the only right way.

My first internship, at an eye care device company, changed how I chose work. I stopped asking how much a role paid and started asking whose life it changes. Since then I've worked across medical devices, clinical research and a tech startup, usually in the gap between the people doing the work and the technology meant to help them.
