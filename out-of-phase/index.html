Chapter 7 · About

The project behind the game.

Out of Phase tests whether batteries that chase the market price help or hurt the local grid, in Australia and India, using real data.

What we found

Two real days, one in Australia and one in India, run through an optimiser that knows the whole day in advance.

How to play

Each hour, make one move.

Charge

Buy power to fill your battery. It costs money, and the street gets busier while you charge.

Hold

Do nothing. Keep your power saved for later.

Release

Sell power from your battery. You earn money, and the street gets a break.

Look at the strip of hours at the bottom. Red hours are when the street is overloaded, so release then. Charge in cheap, quiet hours before them. Not sure? Press the glowing button. It shows a good move for this hour.
