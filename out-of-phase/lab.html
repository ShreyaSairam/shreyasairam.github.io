How to play

  1. Each turn is one hour. Choose Charge, Hold or Release.
  2. Charge buys power. It costs money and adds load to the street.
  3. Release sells power. It earns money and takes load off the street.
  4. Keep the street under its red strain line, and earn as much as you can. The forecast shows what is coming.
  5. End the day with the battery full again, or you pay to refill it.

    What we found

    Two grids, two different answers. Large battery, real days.
    Australia

    India

    In both countries

    Chasing the price can leave the street worse off than no battery.

    The cheapest time to charge is often midday, which is exactly when city centres are busiest. A battery that only follows the price adds load right when the wires are already working hardest.

    What would fix it

    Pay batteries for the hours that matter locally.

    • Australia: a small network payment for releasing during local peaks would tip batteries towards doing both.
    • India: state tenders could add local peak duties to their dispatch rules, and price them in.
    • Both: better forecasts of local peaks and prices make "both" possible on more days.

    How it works

    Real data, simple rules, one optimiser.
    The data
    • Sydney: NSW market prices from AEMO and the City South zone substation load from Ausgrid, every 15 minutes, 29 Feb 2024.
    • Delhi: India's day-ahead exchange prices from IEX and Delhi's city load from Delhi SLDC, every 15 minutes, 19 Jun 2024.
    • IEX prices via the Global Day-Ahead Electricity Price dataset (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0).
    The rules
    • Strain line: the busiest 1% of that place's year. It marks a network at its hardest, not a failure point.
    • Street strain: energy above the line, compared with the same day without a battery. Over 100% means the battery made it worse.
    • Battery: power set as a share of the local peak, 4 hours of storage, 90% round trip, starts and ends the day full.
    The optimiser
    • "Balance it for me" solves for the most money possible while keeping street strain under a limit set by your slider.
    • It knows the whole day in advance, so it shows the best case. Real batteries must forecast prices and load.
    • Balance score: the square root of money kept times the calm left on the street, from 0 to 100.
